The system can learn usage patterns and automatically switch devices based on routine.

Project Vision

Beyond control toward intelligence

The long-term goal is not only to remotely control a light, but to create a home that understands context, predicts needs, and responds in a safe and efficient way. That is the real promise of smart home engineering.

  • Predictive automation based on time and occupancy
  • Voice-triggered scenes such as study mode or night mode
  • Sensor-driven security and environmental monitoring
  • Cloud dashboards for analytics and remote management
